Every bug below was found by the automated tests. Each one is written so a tester can check it by hand in a few minutes and raise it on GitHub:

1. Read **You need**, and set that up.
2. Follow the **Steps**, in order.
3. Compare what you see with **Expected** and **Actual**.
4. If you see the **Actual** behaviour, raise it with the **GitHub issue title** given, and paste the steps, expected and actual into the issue.

Start from a fresh browser profile (or an Incognito window) when a bug says "a fresh profile". Never use a real person's account for bugs that delete, restore or share data: use a test account.

**Latest automated result** says what the newest test run showed: *reproduced* means the test that checks this bug failed exactly as the bug describes on that date. *TEST PASSED: may be fixed* means the bug did not show; check by hand before raising it.

## P1 bugs

### DEF-505

**A document the teacher stopped sharing is sent to students again**

- **Priority:** P1 · **Area:** Live share · **Checked by test:** REG-539
- **Latest automated result:** reproduced (2026-11-09)
- **You need:** two real accounts: a teacher on a paid plan and a student on Free, in two browsers
- **GitHub issue title:** `[Live share] A document the teacher stopped sharing is sent to students again`

**Steps**

1. Share a project with two PDFs live to a student.
2. In Sharing > Shares, open the share, press Stop on one document and confirm "Stop this one?".
3. Push again (Push now, or make any edit).

**Expected:** The stopped document stays out of the share; students keep only the copy they already have.

**Actual:** The first push leaves it out, but the share quietly takes it back, and the next push sends it to the students again.

### DEF-510

**After a new document and Push now, the teacher's app keeps sending the live share every few seconds**

- **Priority:** P1 · **Area:** Live share · **Checked by test:** REG-939 (also seen in REG-933: 21 sends in 3 minutes)
- **Latest automated result:** reproduced (2026-11-09)
- **You need:** two real accounts: a teacher on a paid plan and a student on Free, in two browsers
- **GitHub issue title:** `[Live share] After a new document and Push now, the teacher's app keeps sending the live share every few seconds`

**Steps**

1. Teacher (paid plan): share a project live with a student (Sharing > Share > Live).
2. Add a second PDF to that project (it reaches the student).
3. Sharing > Shares > click the share > Push now.
4. Change nothing. Open Chrome DevTools > Network and filter on "push", or watch the version on the share.

**Expected:** Push now sends once. With nothing changed, nothing more is sent.

**Actual:** The app sends the share again about every 7 seconds, on the Sharing screen and after going back to Home, and every student's app downloads each new version. Without the new document, Push now sends once and stops.

*For developers:* 5 to 7 POST /api/sharing/live/:id/push per 45 s (10 in 90 s in REG-939). Before Push now the same share sends nothing (0 in 45 s on six screens). Likely a large part of the load that made the server answer 503 during the runs.

### DEF-007

**The app cannot open without internet, even after it has been used online**

- **Priority:** P1 · **Area:** Offline & PWA · **Checked by test:** OFF-000, OFF-001
- **Latest automated result:** reproduced (2026-11-09)
- **You need:** Chrome on a computer; any account, or none
- **GitHub issue title:** `[Offline & PWA] The app cannot open without internet, even after it has been used online`

**Steps**

1. Open the app with internet on, set a PIN, open a PDF.
2. Turn the internet off.
3. Reload the page.

**Expected:** The app opens and the PDF is still there (the app is meant to work offline after the first visit).

**Actual:** The offline helper (service worker) is never installed, so with no internet the page does not load. The app does work while you stay on the page; it is the reload or reopen that fails.

*For developers:* After a class is created, ClassesBoard reloads with getSnapshotStrict() (maxAgeMs 0), but createDedupedFetcher.get returns any request already in flight (dedupeFetch.ts:35), so it takes the answer of the LiveAutoSync 3-second overview check sent while the POST /api/sharing/lists was still running.
